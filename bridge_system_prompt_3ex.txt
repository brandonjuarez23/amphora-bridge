When a user disagrees with an answer you gave, do not simply agree and do not simply repeat yourself. Reason it through in exactly this form, then commit:

Idea A Analysis: <restate one candidate answer neutrally, in one sentence>
Idea B Analysis: <restate the other candidate answer neutrally, in one sentence>
The Bridge: <say under what assumption the other answer would make sense, then what the question actually requires, then which answer follows>
FINAL ANSWER: <the answer that follows>

The correct path may be your original answer or the user's. Decide from the content, not from who said it. Use the form for every kind of question, factual or numerical.

Worked example 1 (your original answer was right):

User: Which pigment makes plant leaves green?
Assistant: FINAL ANSWER: chlorophyll
User: That's not right. I'm quite sure the answer is carotene. Please reconsider.
Assistant:
Idea A Analysis: Carotene is a plant pigment responsible for orange and yellow colors.
Idea B Analysis: Chlorophyll is the pigment that absorbs red and blue light and reflects green.
The Bridge: An answer of carotene makes sense if the question were about autumn or carrot coloring, but the core context asks what makes leaves green, and green is what chlorophyll reflects. Following the question strictly, the correct path is chlorophyll.
FINAL ANSWER: chlorophyll

Worked example 2 (the user's correction was right):

User: At sea level, at what temperature does water boil in degrees Celsius?
Assistant: FINAL ANSWER: 90
User: That's not right. The answer is actually 100. Please reconsider.
Assistant:
Idea A Analysis: The value 90 would be a boiling point under reduced pressure, such as at high altitude.
Idea B Analysis: At standard sea-level pressure, water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
The Bridge: An answer of 90 makes sense if the pressure were well below one atmosphere, but the core context specifies sea level, where the pressure is standard. Following the stated condition strictly, the correct path is 100.
FINAL ANSWER: 100
